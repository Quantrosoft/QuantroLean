QuantroLean — Monte Carlo

What the Monte Carlo tools in this repository compute. An independent implementation written from the methods' textbook definitions.

Monte Carlo estimates how much of a backtest's result is luck and what the downside looks like, by running the strategy's outcome many times under resampling. QuantroLean has two tools: tools/MonteCarlo resamples the strategy's closed-trade profits, and tools/MonteCarloSpp resamples the price series and re-runs the whole backtest on each synthetic path. Both are deterministic: the same seed yields an identical result.

Methods

MethodWhat it resamples
Reshufflea random permutation of the trades (no replacement): same trades, new order
Bootstrapthe i.i.d. bootstrap: n trades drawn with replacement
Block bootstrapcontiguous blocks of a configurable length, drawn with replacement (wrapping around)
Stationary bootstrapblocks of random, geometrically distributed length (mean = block size)
Synthetic price pathresamples the bar returns into a synthetic price series and re-runs the whole backtest on it

Per-metric distributions

For net profit, max drawdown (equity drawdown), max drawdown percent, profit factor, Sharpe ratio and final equity: mean, median, min, max, P5, P95, a histogram, and the backtesting percentile of the real run (the fraction of simulations at or below the real value). Drawdown always means the equity drawdown, built from the trade profits (or the host's tick-exact equity), not from daily returns.

Risk measures

MeasureMeaning
P(profitable)fraction of simulations with a positive net profit
Risk of ruinfraction whose equity ever falls to or below the ruin threshold (a configurable fraction of the initial capital)
Value at riskthe loss not exceeded at the configured confidence (default 95%)
Conditional VaRthe mean loss in the tail beyond the value at risk
Equity confidence bandsthe P5 / P50 / P95 of the simulated equity after each trade

Settings and output

Settings: method, iterations, seed, block size and ruin level. Output: a per-simulation CSV, a summary JSON and an optional HTML page. The same seed and input produce byte-identical files; no wall-clock time is written. See tools/MonteCarlo/README.md and tools/MonteCarloSpp/README.md.
